There is more
below the surface.

01Giants, sustained by the smallest life+

A manta's immense size hides a delicate way of feeding. Cephalic fins help guide water toward its mouth, where plankton is filtered out. Following these animals means following the changing productivity of the ocean itself.

Read the NOAA species profile ↗
02The ocean has a language+

Orcas use calls to communicate and echolocation to explore their surroundings. Their close social groups pass behaviours between generations. Different populations can have different diets and ways of hunting.

Explore orca lives with NOAA ↗
03Light where sunlight cannot reach+

In the deep ocean, some animals produce their own light through a chemical reaction. Bioluminescence can help them defend themselves, find food, or communicate. Our Medusa portrait celebrates this living light; it is an artistic study, not an identification of a particular species.
