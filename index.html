Mobula birostris

Giant
manta rays

Giant manta rays use broad, wing-like fins to move through tropical and subtropical seas. They filter plankton from the water.

Family

Diet

How manta rays feed+

Manta rays filter plankton from seawater. The fins beside their mouths, called cephalic fins, help guide water in.

Orca calls and hunting+

Orcas use calls to communicate and echolocation to explore their surroundings. Family groups pass behaviours between generations. Different populations can have different diets and ways of hunting.

Why some ocean animals glow+

Bioluminescence is light produced by a chemical reaction. Some ocean animals use it to defend themselves, find food or communicate.
